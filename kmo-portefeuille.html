KMO-portefeuille: what is still possible in 2026?

The rules have changed. We prefer to be precise about what may qualify before you plan a project around a subsidy.

Advice is now limited to cybersecurity

Since 1 February 2026, advice is only eligible within the cybersecurity theme. General brand advice, brand strategy and general AI consultancy therefore no longer qualify for support through the KMO-portefeuille.

Training can still qualify

Eligible training can still receive support when the programme, provider and participant meet the current conditions. Glossy does not promise eligibility automatically: ask us about the specific training you have in mind and always verify the final conditions with VLAIO.

Read the current rules on the official VLAIO website.
