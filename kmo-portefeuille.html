KMO-portefeuille

Subsidies for consultancy & strategy.

To build a substantiated brand, it is essential to begin with a strategic communication approach. Flemish SMEs can leverage KMO-portefeuille subsidies for brand consultancy & brand strategy. This means that for the initial stages of the (re)branding process, you may receive up to a 30% discount, with a maximum level of annual aid that is set at €7,500.

Visit the Agentschap Innoveren & Ondernemen website for more information and full details on eligibility requirements.
