The tools are everywhere. Judgement is not.

The models are the easy part. Someone still has to know what to make, what to skip and what good looks like for your brand.

Glossy has spent more than twenty years making that call for brands like KAA Gent, Pukkelpop and Quick-Step. The challenge

Everyone is prompting. Nobody is steering.

Most organisations do not have an AI problem. They have a clarity problem.

We see the same picture in most teams we meet: designers in Midjourney, a copywriter pasting briefs into a free ChatGPT account, the best prompts living in one colleague's private notes and three subscriptions nobody remembers ordering. Output goes up. Consistency goes down. And no one can say which tools are allowed to see customer data.

The answer is rarely another tool, and it is never a forty-page strategy deck. It is a working method: agreed tools, shared prompts, clear review moments and one written definition of what on-brand means, tested on real work and owned by your team.

Our point of view

AI is bigger than AI.

A model does not know your brand, your customer or your taste.

It does not know what it should not make. That knowledge sits with people, and it is exactly what we bring to every AI decision: what the technology can genuinely do today, what it cannot and when the honest answer is that you do not need it.

This is also our own story. AI collapsed the distance between an idea and a working product, and it changed our craft: the designer became the strategist and the developer. In 2023 we were generating images. Today we design propositions, train image models on brand identities and build the databases, generators and websites we ship. Glossy did not add AI to the menu. We rebuilt the studio around it.

The deep dive

Taking it on stage

What we do

Advise. Build. Make. Teach.

Concrete work, named tools and systems your team keeps.

01.

Advise

Where AI genuinely pays off in your marketing, and where it does not. Workflow audits, an opportunity map with owners and deadlines, tool choices with the reasoning written down and practical guardrails (not legal advice): which data may enter which tool, and where the EU AI Act touches marketing work.

02.

Build

Repeatable systems your team runs without us: content workflows from brief to publication, prompt libraries that live in your tools rather than in someone's head, custom assistants, databases and small internal apps. Everything is documented and handed over, with support afterwards if you want it. You own it.

03.

Make

Brand-locked image and video production. We train models on your identity: ComfyUI, Flux and LoRAs when control matters; Midjourney, Krea and Runway when speed does. The output looks like your brand, not like AI. The BattMobility image pipeline is a working example.

04.

Teach

From a one-day team workshop to a four-evening masterclass, with the same material Ben teaches at the School of Branding: what these systems really do, hands-on practice on your own cases and the honest limits.
