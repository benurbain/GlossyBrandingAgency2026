The challenge

Everyone is prompting. Nobody is steering.

Most organisations do not have an AI problem. They have a clarity problem.

We see the same picture in most teams we meet: designers in Midjourney, a copywriter pasting briefs into a free ChatGPT account, the best prompts living in one colleague's private notes and three subscriptions nobody remembers ordering. Output goes up. Consistency goes down. And no one can say which tools are allowed to see customer data.

The answer is rarely another tool, and it is never a forty-page strategy deck. It is a working method: agreed tools, shared prompts, clear review moments and one written definition of what on-brand means, tested on real work and owned by your team.

Who this is for

For teams caught between AI pressure and brand responsibility.

You do not need more inspiration. You need a practical way forward.

  • Your team is experimenting, but everyone works differently and nothing is written down.
  • Content volume is rising while quality and consistency slip.
  • Leadership expects an AI plan, and the business case is still vague.
  • You want speed without your brand starting to look like everyone else's AI output.
  • You want guidance from people who build, not another management layer.

If you are looking for a hundred-page transformation deck or a promise that AI will replace your people, we are the wrong call. If one tool solves your problem, we will tell you so in the first meeting.

Our point of view

AI is bigger than AI.

A model does not know your brand, your customer or your taste.

It does not know what it should not make. That knowledge sits with people, and it is exactly what we bring to every AI decision: what the technology can genuinely do today, what it cannot and when the honest answer is that you do not need it.

This is also our own story. AI collapsed the distance between an idea and a working product, and it changed our craft: the designer became the strategist and the developer. In 2023 we were generating images. Today we design propositions, train image models on brand identities and build the databases, generators and websites we ship. Glossy did not add AI to the menu. We rebuilt the studio around it.

Proof of work

How our practice evolved.

From brand work to AI in daily practice.

2016

Branding AI before building with it

We branded Miia, a San Francisco company working on text understanding, and later Miles Ahead, the Deep Tech & AI venture studio that went on to raise €20 million. The tools came later; the sector fluency started here.

2023

The deep dive

A year inside the open-source stack (ComfyUI, LoRAs, IP-Adapters and, later, Flux), training image models on brand identities instead of prompting and hoping. Prompt engineering became a Glossy service, and Ben started lecturing at the School of Branding, Artevelde's postgraduate programme.

2024

Taking it on stage

We co-founded Prompt Up The Jam™: four teams, one hour, AI tools and a live audience vote, staged with partners like NTGent and S.M.A.K. Ben closed the year with a School of Branding talk on what LLMs can and cannot do for branding.

2025

From prompting to shipping

A four-evening Generative AI Masterclass for 46 professionals, together with Arteveldehogeschool. We built the free Video Bento Generator, sketched in Figma and iterated to version 43 with ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini, plus a client-specific branded video generator for reusable social content. The Bento write-up is on our news page.

2026

Client work, AI inside

For BattMobility we combine classic strategy work (propositions, B2B and B2C communication) with AI-built prototypes, marketing apps and a brand-trained image pipeline. We rebuilt this website ourselves with AI as pair-programmer, including the pipeline that generates all 54 case pages. And we publish what changes in our stack, including why we sidelined Midjourney for more than six months, and why we brought it back.
