Cookie policy

As our website uses cookies, we would like to inform you about the different types of cookies we use, which cookies our service providers use, and how you can manage and block cookies.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of data that a website asks your browser to store on your computer, laptop or mobile device when you visit a website or a certain page of that website. The cookie enables that website to remember your actions and preferences over time and to apply them during subsequent visits. An example of such a preference is your preferred language.

Cookies usually contain the name of the website from which the cookie came, how long the cookie will remain on your device (the expiration date) and a value which is usually a randomly generated unique number.

A distinction is made between strictly necessary cookies and other cookies. The strictly necessary cookies are needed for the proper functioning of the website, for example for its security and accessibility. Under applicable law, we are not obliged to ask for your consent for the use of such cookies. We will ask for your consent for each of the other categories.

Cookies can be divided into the following categories:

  • Permanent and session cookies. A permanent cookie recognises the web browser on repeated visits to the website; a session cookie recognises the web browser during a single visit. Permanent cookies are saved on your device, whereas session cookies are deleted at the end of every session.
  • First-party and third-party cookies. First-party cookies are placed by the website itself. Third-party cookies are placed by other websites; this happens when our website contains elements of other websites, such as social media plugins or advertisements.
  • Performance and functional cookies. Performance cookies track how a visitor uses the website, which allows us to adapt and optimise its content and usability. Functional cookies remember your preferences, such as the language you chose, making the website more user friendly.

Cookies will not always contain personal data, but they can contain information that leads to your identification, in which case such a cookie is treated as personal data and this privacy policy applies.
