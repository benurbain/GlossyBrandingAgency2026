Rebranding of an online video animation software platform

About the case

Moovly, with its impressive track record, has revolutionized the world of online content creation in under 5 years. Their platform enables users to effortlessly create videos, presentations, e-cards, and more, all from scratch. Seeking to redefine their go-to-market strategy and overall identity, Moovly approached Glossy Branding Agency for a transformative solution.

In today's landscape, where convenience, stunning results, and deep insights are paramount for users, Glossy Branding Agency devised a strategy for Moovly to lead the way as an expert and thought leader. By positioning Moovly as a product brand rather than just a company, with an effortless and digitally-focused identity, Moovly aims to flip the funnel and prioritize the user experience. It's all about providing an affordable, intuitive, and simple cloud-based platform that empowers everyone to create captivating animated videos.
