BattMobility

Freedom in Motion

All cases

01 · The challenge

From a bold mobility idea to a brand people trust.

How do you turn shared electric mobility for everyone into a business that scales? BattMobility set out to give communities freedom of movement without the burden of car ownership. Our job: shape the strategy, build the brand, and engineer the digital tools that bring Freedom in Motion to life.

Chris de Guytenaer
Company
BattMobility

Location
Ghent, Belgium

02 · Strategy

Strategy &
go-to-market

Every strong brand begins with clarity. Through research and stakeholder sessions we defined BattMobility’s purpose, positioning and proposition, then translated it into a concrete go-to-market strategy: who to reach first, what to promise, and how to grow. A roadmap that took the brand from concept to a living, growing community.

03 · Identity

The brand
identity

The identity had to feel human, optimistic and effortlessly modern: mobility that belongs to everyone. We designed a flexible visual language built around motion and energy, with a fresh colour palette and confident typography that works from a phone screen to the side of a vehicle.

04 · System

The design
system

To keep everything consistent as the brand scaled, we built the BattMobility 2025 Style Guide. Colour, type, spacing, components, iconography and rules for every touchpoint were captured as reusable tokens and templates, so the team can create on-brand material at speed, without us in the room.

05 · Imagery

AI-driven
imagery

Photography at scale is slow and expensive. We developed a bespoke AI imagery pipeline to generate on-brand visuals: people, places and vehicles in the BattMobility world. It gives the brand an endless, consistent and ownable image library for campaigns, social and product.

06 · Tools

Custom-built apps:
the Marketing OS

Great brands need great tools. We engineered a suite of custom applications, with a Marketing OS at its core, including a socials template builder, an email builder and more. The team now produces branded social posts and emails in minutes, fully on-system, turning the design system into living software.

07 · Results

Real momentum,
built to keep scaling.

2,0003,000users

Strategy, brand and tooling working as one system produced a 50% increase, with a brand and a toolset built to keep scaling. Freedom in Motion, delivered.
