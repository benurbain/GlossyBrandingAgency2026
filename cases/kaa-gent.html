About the case

Introducing the new era of KAA Gent, the iconic soccer team since 1895, affectionately known as the "BUFFALO'S" since 1924. Glossy Branding Agency took up the challenge to create a powerful team identity centered around the inspiring slogan, "The future starts here."

With a rich history symbolized by the enduring indian logo, Glossy Branding Agency crafted a strong and modern brand that captures the spirit of the club. Notably, KAA Gent clinched the national championship in the 2014-2015 season, solidifying their place in the hearts of fans.

Get ready to cheer on the Buffalo's as they lead the way to a bright future. Buffalo!
