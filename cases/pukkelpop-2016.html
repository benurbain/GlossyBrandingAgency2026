About the case

Glossy Branding Agency provided the brand update for the 2016 edition of Pukkelpop, Belgium's largest "Alternative" festival. With a focus on improving the festival's online and offline presence, we meticulously developed a comprehensive style guide that provided clear brand direction to third parties.

Our involvement extended to various aspects of the festival's marketing and promotion. Glossy Branding Agency provided art direction for social media and ensured a cohesive visual identity across all platforms. In addition, we were responsible for the creation of the festival's website, promotional videos, eye-catching site branding and engaging video walls.
