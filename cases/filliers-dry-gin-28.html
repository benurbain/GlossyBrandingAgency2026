A new Belgian Gin with 28 botanicals

About the case

Filliers Dry Gin 28, a handcrafted premium gin with a rich heritage, has been a hallmark of excellence since 1880. Infused and distilled with 28 meticulously selected botanicals, this gin is a testament to the artistry of the Master Distiller at Filliers Distillery.

In 2011, Glossy Branding Agency had the privilege of collaborating with Filliers Distillery to develop and design a new product. With a carefully crafted strategic plan, a new gin was brought to life under the guidance of Bernard Filliers. The result was a gin that embodied the essence of craftsmanship and innovation.

Today, Filliers Dry Gin 28 continues to captivate gin enthusiasts with its exquisite flavors and timeless appeal. It stands as a testament to the partnership between Filliers Distillery and Glossy Branding Agency, combining tradition and modernity to create a truly remarkable gin.
