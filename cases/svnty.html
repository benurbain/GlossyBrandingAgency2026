About the case

When FC70, the renowned Antwerp fashion agency, approached us to reshape their own brand SVNTY, it felt like a dream come true. With great pleasure, we embarked on a journey with founders Miguel & Gregory and their team, aiming to transform SVNTY into a brand that defies conventions. SVNTY: Handy, witty and a touch mischievous. Let us elaborate.

SVNTY is not your average Antwerp fashion brand; it is a statement for women and children who crave a life beyond the ordinary. It encompasses an effortlessly stylish look with a unique attitude. What sets SVNTY apart is its commitment to exceptional quality at accessible prices. Whether you're picking up the kids from school, attending that important lunch, meeting Ryan Gosling or going out, SVNTY makes sure it's always showtime, front and center. Get ready to embrace the unconventional at the edge of the stage.
