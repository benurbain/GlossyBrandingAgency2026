About the case

Foodsie, where it all starts with food, with an emphasis on the complete experience. With a contemporary mindset, they cater to quality seekers looking to elevate their culinary journey. From Kanye's fashion show in Paris to Bill Gates' Zune product launch, Foodsie's team creates customized experiences that leave an unforgettable impression. Whether in downtown Ghent or in your New York penthouse, Foodsie brings it to life.

They are true food nomads, adept at creating the ideal ambiance in any setting - from bustling urban jungles to cozy retreats in the treetops.

Foodsie are creative chefs.
