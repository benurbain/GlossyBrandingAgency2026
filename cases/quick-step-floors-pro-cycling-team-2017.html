A new year, a new identity

About the case

For the 2017 season, Glossy Branding Agency was given the exciting opportunity to develop the comprehensive team identity for the newly formed Quick-Step Pro Cycling Team. Drawing from our passion for cycling, we embarked on a journey to create a fresh and contemporary identity for one of the most renowned teams in the peloton.

From the eye-catching jerseys to the carefully designed stationery and various deliverables, Glossy Branding Agency left no stone unturned to create a cohesive and visually engaging brand experience for the team. With attention to detail and a deep understanding of the cycling world, we ensured that every aspect of the team's identity exuded professionalism and style.

The result is a brand identity that not only reflects the team's iconic status, but also captures the essence of their dynamic and competitive spirit. Glossy Branding Agency is proud to have contributed to the success of the Quick-Step Pro Cycling Team through our design expertise.
