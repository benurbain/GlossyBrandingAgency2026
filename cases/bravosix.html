About the brand

Bravosix is an independent interior design agency based in Ostend, Belgium. Founder Nicolas Delahaye asked us to develop a brand that matches his own personality. A brand in which simplicity is key, but with the proper twist. Based on our thorough strategic ideation sessions, we developed a brand strategy with a strong 'no-nonsense' approach. Bravosix always strives to deliver ideas & designs that become outstanding projects, meeting the high standards of all clients. The brand identity reflects this mindset. We chose a neutral colour palette with orange as a bold accent colour. The orange is echoed in the professional photography. The use of large fonts provides the eye-catcher the brand deserves.
