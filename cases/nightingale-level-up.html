About the case

During Nightingale's Press Days 2015, the exhibition focuses on performance, photography, installation and video, all in the context of fashion, food, technology and design. Nightingale showcases both emerging and established talents pushing the boundaries of traditional disciplines to create memorable experiences.

Glossy Branding Agency was asked to provide appropriate Art Direction and copywriting for this event. We built the right look and feel for an event that brings together the intersections between art, technology and culture. From Upcoming To Established.
