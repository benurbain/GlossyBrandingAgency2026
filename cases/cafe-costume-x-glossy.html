Opaque Rock Solid Exclusive Linings

About the case

The Café Costume x Glossy Branding Agency Opaque linings draw inspiration from the timeless beauty of marble, renowned for its intricate patterns and cultural significance. Marble has been a symbol of tradition and refined taste for centuries, favored by sculptors, architects, and connoisseurs. Its diverse colors and elegant surface make it a luxurious and decorative material. The collaboration between Glossy Branding Agency and Café Costume has produced memorable visuals, linings, and campaigns, reflecting the brand's commitment to quality.

For the 2016 season, Glossy Branding Agency aimed to create a collection of exclusive marble linings, featuring names like Carrara, Onyx, and Negro Marquina, embodying the essence of luxury and sophistication.
