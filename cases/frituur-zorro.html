About the Brand

Meet Zorro - a stylish 'frituur' in Ghent's student neighborhood. Our brand embodies adventure and passion for freshly prepared food. With Zorro's iconic mask & hat as our logo, we promise a memorable dining experience. Get together with friends in our cozy ambiance and enjoy the aroma of sizzling fries. Come to Frituur Zorro, where stylish vibes, flavorful fries and friendly service await you.

Let your taste buds run wild in the heart of Ghent's student district. Experience Frituur Zorro - a taste adventure freshly made in the hacienda!
