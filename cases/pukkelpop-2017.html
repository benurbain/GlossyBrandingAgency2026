About the case

Welcome home to Pukkelpop! Glossy Branding Agency took the developed branding from 2015 and transformed it into an exciting new campaign. Pukkelpop is not just about music; it's about the collective experience. Together we create the vibrant atmosphere that makes it feel like home.

Glossy Branding Agency was once again commissioned to create the full look and feel of Pukkelpop, both online and offline. For the 2017 edition, we embraced the power of video walls to enhance on-site communication. We created a comprehensive video style guide, allowing third parties to seamlessly integrate their branding into the overall visual experience.

Partners such as Proximus, Alken-Maes, Coca-Cola, Lipton Ice Tea, Humo and many others found it effortless to align their branding with the integrated communications strategy. We look forward to seeing you again next year at Pukkelpop, where we will continue to create unforgettable moments together.
