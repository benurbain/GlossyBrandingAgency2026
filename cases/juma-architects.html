About the case

In 2016, JUMA Architects wanted to highlight their completed projects, and Glossy Branding Agency responded by developing a pure and simple look and feel for online and offline platforms.

JUMA Architects, founded by Mathieu Luyens and Julie van De Keere in 2009, is a Ghent-based firm known for its focus on creating well-designed and customized structures. With a modern and minimalist aesthetic, they skillfully integrate light, space and emotion, with careful attention to the unique characteristics of each site. Their mission is to efficiently guide projects from the initial planning stages to the meticulous detailing of materials and furniture design. Through a specific approach that combines passionate design and functional construction, JUMA Architects effectively creates cost-effective and personalized structures for their clients.

With a commitment to excellence and a deep understanding of the intersection between design and functionality, JUMA Architects is ready to transform your architectural vision into reality.
