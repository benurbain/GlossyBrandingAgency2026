About the case

If you're looking for an extraordinary Italian dining experience in the heart of historic Ghent, look no further than Barbaro. They pride themselves on being anything but a tourist trap. Prepare for exquisite antipasti and pizzas that will amaze you. Barbaro's creations are not only visually stunning, but also excite your senses with their delicious flavors.

At Barbaro, they believe that to bend the rules, you must first know the rules. Prepare for a culinary journey that defies expectations and delivers an unforgettable taste sensation.
