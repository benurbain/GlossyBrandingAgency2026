About the case

For over 30 years Pukkelpop selects a musical line-up with an alternative fringe. The festival has become one of Europe’s greatest outdoor music events with more than 250 current musical sensations, living legends and visionary alternative artists. It’s fun, relaxed, varied and very much built around the love of music!
