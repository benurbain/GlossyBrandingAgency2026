Adding Gold

About the case

Nightingale, an experimental marketing agency based in Antwerp and Berlin, worked with us to develop a comprehensive communication strategy and digital campaign for AW 2014. Titled "Adding Gold," the initiative served as a catalyst for increased brand awareness both online and offline.

The campaign did well in several magazines in Belgium and abroad and strengthened Nightingale's presence. Nightingale gained remarkable exposure and left an indelible mark on the marketing landscape.
