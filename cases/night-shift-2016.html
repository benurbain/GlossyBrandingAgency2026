After Hours Business Lab

About the case

For Night Shift 2016, we developed The After Hours Business Lab, a concept that reinforces the creative dimension of entrepreneurship - a playground where experimentation fuels innovation. We encourage risk-taking and foster an environment where trying something new has no consequences.

Night Shift is the catalyst that motivates and encourages such entrepreneurial endeavors. We have carefully developed an alluring and sophisticated look and feel that seamlessly integrates with each of Night Shift's communication channels, ensuring a consistent and high-quality look and feel.
