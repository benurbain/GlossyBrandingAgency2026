About the brand

Meet Mòris, the new bar in the center of Ghent. With a focus on unforgettable nights and premium drinks, Mòris promises "The Perfect Bliss." Join us for an exquisite experience with carefully curated drinks that excite the taste buds. Located in the heart of the city, Mòris is the destination of choice for creating fond memories with friends.

Cheers to extraordinary evenings at Mòris, where premium drinks and delicious company come together to create magic.
