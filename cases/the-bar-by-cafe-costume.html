Brand Identity for The Bar by Café Costume

About the case

Introducing Café Costume's new venue, a stylish bar nestled in the heart of Antwerp. With its abundance of marble, we crafted a captivating identity that embodies the venue's allure and sophistication. Our design concept captures the essence of this contemporary space, merging elegance with modernity. From the sleek logo to the refined branding elements, every detail reflects the bar's unique ambiance.
