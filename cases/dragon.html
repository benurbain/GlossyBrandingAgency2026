About the brand

Welcome to Dragòn, an "International Premium Beer Café" in the heart of Ghent. Our bar is located in the historic center in Klein Turkey, just opposite the Sint-Niklaaskerk, situated on the Korenmarkt in Ghent.

In addition to 30 beers from Belgian brewers on tap, we also offer delicious Dierendonck food pairing as well as mind-blowing beer cocktails & boiler makers. You can also taste a selection of unique beers that mature in our maturation cabinet. Don't forget our own Dragòn beer in barrel!
