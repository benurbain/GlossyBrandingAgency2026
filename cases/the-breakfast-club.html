Brand Identity for a production company

About the case

Inspired by the iconic 80's film, The Breakfast Club, our client has created a real-life haven where individuals from diverse backgrounds converge. They proudly call themselves a production company, uniting publicity agencies, creatives, directors, photographers, and brands in a shared space of curiosity, collaboration, and creation.

We had the privilege of crafting their new identity, a visual representation of their vibrant community. With their trust in our expertise, we designed a brand that captures the spirit of inclusivity, innovation, and boundless imagination that thrives within The Breakfast Club.
