Unveiling the new face of Histoires d'O

A new identity, menus and a revamped website

We are delighted to unveil the complete rebranding of Histoires d'O, the charming seaside restaurant in Ostend. With a brand new identity, revamped menus and an updated website, Histoires d'O is ready to strengthen its position as the destination for delicious apéros and creative cuisine.

With a new colour combination straight from the golden hour on their terrace, we went to work to give the brand a warm and contemporary look. We also created exclusive posters of Ostend for the first visitors, to give them a memento of their visit.
