De Verloren Kost - A place for experiments in work and pleasure

We took charge of the art direction, design, and website development for De Verloren Kost

De Verloren Kost is a new society located at the Coupure in Ghent. This society is a haven for creative individuals seeking an inspiring work environment and an opportunity to actively contribute to our agenda.

Members of De Verloren Kost have access to a space that combines work and pleasure, while also playing an active role in shaping our events.

On the ground floor, several flexible spaces are available for work and meetings. The founders believe that surrounding oneself with beautiful things enhances the work experience. That's why interior architect Caroline De Wolf (Casa Argentaurum) designed De Verloren Kost, showcasing a rotating collection of art objects on a permanent basis.

In the sous-terrain, members can unwind in the lounge and bar area, which also serves as the backdrop for most of our delightful activities.
