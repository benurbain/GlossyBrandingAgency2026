Inspiring young marketing potentials at Artevelde Academy

Our account manager Sam gave a guest lecture at Artevelde Academy

On Wednesday 19 April 2023, our account manager Sam gave a guest lecture at Artevelde Academy about our branding work to the marketers of the future. The focus was on all the different aspects of branding and how branding lays the foundation for organisations, products or services. Sam also talked about what it is like to work at an agency and tried to convince these young marketing potentials to consider applying for jobs in the amazing world of agencies.

Fun fact: Sam started his journey at Glossy Branding Agency after attending the guest lecture about branding by our founder Ben Urbain during his studies at the Artevelde Academy. The circle is complete!

We thank Artevelde Academy & Nathalie De Boeck for the invitation.
