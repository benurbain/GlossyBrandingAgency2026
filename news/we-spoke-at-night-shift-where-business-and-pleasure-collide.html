We spoke at Night Shift: Where Business and Pleasure Collide

An Unforgettable Co-Creation Platform and Networking Event

For the fifth consecutive year, Night Shift is bringing together entrepreneurs in Belgium for a unique approach to doing business. Through their transformative magic, multiple locations in Belgium are turned into vibrant co-creation platforms. This event seamlessly blends start-ups and established businesses, both local and international, while adding a touch of excitement with a central dance floor.

What sets them apart is their commitment to mixing business with pleasure. Indulge your senses with delectable local catering and enjoy a refreshing drink at one of our many bars.

Join us this Saturday at Night Shift, where we expertly blend business with pleasure. And don't miss the opportunity to be inspired by our creative director, Ben Urbain, as he delivers an insightful talk on branding. Catch the man, the legend, the voice live at 21:00h sharp! #brandbrand
