Febelhair's Fresh Digital Facelift: A Portal for Belgian Hairdressers

Online Identity for Febelhair

In collaboration with the Belgian Hairdressers Federation, Febelhair, Glossy Branding Agency proudly presents a revamped online identity. We've crafted a future-proof portal website aimed at guiding and informing professional hairdressers across Belgium.

The goal was clear: to equip Febelhair for the future with a modern UX design and a soft, inviting color palette. These changes ensure that users can effortlessly navigate the content-rich website, making it a valuable resource for Belgian hairdressers.

But our commitment to Febelhair didn't stop there. We really enjoyed to capture the essence of the Febelhair office and team, infusing the website with a personal touch to make the website more welcoming to new members.
