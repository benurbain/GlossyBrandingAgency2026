Crafting a Timeless Brand Strategy for Hotel Du Parc

Brand strategy for the most iconic Art-deco hotel in the heart of Ostend

We are delighted to finally share our collaboration with the iconic Hotel Du Parc in Ostend. Together with Shannah Zeebroek, Nicolas Linden and Ruth Vanden Berghe, we have redefined the essence of this historic art deco gem.

Welcome to Hotel Du Parc, one of the most iconic places in Ostend since 1929. Our mission was clear: capture the hotel's rich heritage and translate it into a brand strategy that promises an unforgettable experience.

From stylish rooms to culinary delights, Hotel Du Parc excites the senses. Whether enjoying a gourmet lunch or an aperitif, you are just steps away from the beautiful coastline.

Our collaboration highlights Hotel Du Parc's iconic heritage while giving it a contemporary vitality. It remains a symbol of sophistication, where guests can escape the ordinary and embrace the extraordinary.

Experience the blend of history, art and modern elegance at Hotel Du Parc, a coastal treasure since 1929. We enjoyed a delicious lunch on the terrace, and we must admit, it is more than worth it!
