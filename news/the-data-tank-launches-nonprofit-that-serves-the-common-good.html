The Data Tank Launches Nonprofit that serves the common good

We created the brand identity for The Data Tank, a nonprofit that serves the common good by using data differently

On Open Data Day - march 7, The Data Tank was launched. Together with their team, Glossy created a remarkable branding that helps them to achieve their goal to serve the common good by using data differently.

We wholeheartedly congratulate CEO Dr. Julia Stamm, co-founder Dr. Stefaan Verhulst, Board Secretary Wim De Waele, Chairman Luc Tayart de Borms and the entire Data Tank Team on the launch of this exciting nonprofit venture. It truly has the power to revolutionize the use and accessibility of data, benefiting people from all walks of life.

We are eagerly looking forward to the future and are excited about the challenges and opportunities arising from the data-driven transformation of today's society.
