School Of Branding 2024-2025

Glossy Branding Agency Joins the 2024-2025 School of Branding Cycle (Again)

We’re excited to announce that Glossy Branding Agency will be a proud participant in the upcoming 2024-2025 School of Branding Year! For those passionate about graphic design and branding, the School of Branding is the perfect place to learn the ins and outs of the industry, from strategic thinking to visual design, and even how to launch your own brand.

This prestigious program brings together the best in the business to share their expertise, and we’re honored to join an incredible lineup of industry leaders, including Base Design, Skinn, WeWantMore, and many others. At Glossy Branding Agency, we’re committed to shaping the next generation of branding experts, and we’re thrilled to be part of this initiative.

Through hands-on workshops, expert insights, and real-world projects, students will get the chance to learn from the best. Whether it’s building a brand from scratch or mastering the latest design trends, the School of Branding offers the tools and inspiration to turn creative passion into professional success.

Stay tuned as we share our experience and insights throughout the School of Branding year, because we believe in investing in the future of branding, one designer at a time.
