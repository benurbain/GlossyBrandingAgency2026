Meet Street League 9000, Ghent's neighbourhood football network!

More opportunities for children & young people with the Street League 9000 initiative

Our client KAA Gent Foundation asked us to create the identity for a new football network in Ghent that gives children & young people more opportunities by playing football with them on various squares in the city.

Street League 9000 is formed by the KAA Gent Foundation, Futsal Besiktas, vzw Jong, FC Rooigem, Futsal Gent, vzw Ergenekon and vzw Sportaround.

We are delighted to have been able to contribute to wonderful initiative!
