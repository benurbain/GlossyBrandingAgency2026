New work for Live is Live • An Antwerp city music festival

Returning to the roots of music festivals

We are excited to be working for Live is Live - an Antwerp city music festival. Together with the FKP Scorpio team, we worked on a new brand strategy. We also updated the current art direction to bring the brand vividly to life.

Experience the magic of Live is Live, a three-day music festival in Middenvijverpark, Linkeroever, Antwerp. Immerse yourself in good vibes, good times, and world-class performances. Start your summer with an authentic lineup, friends, and a laid-back festival atmosphere. Enjoy legendary acts and ultimate comfort for a unique, carefree total experience.

Rediscover the original festival vibe and let’s go back to the festival experience without all the extra fuss.

Live is Live is on a mission to redefine the festival experience for music lovers who are looking for added value. They are all about delivering an exceptional journey where the visitors and the music are at the centre. With a relentless focus on hassle-free enjoyment, comfort and attention to detail, Live is Live wants to return to the roots of music festivals.

The new website has been launched, stay tuned for the full line-up!
