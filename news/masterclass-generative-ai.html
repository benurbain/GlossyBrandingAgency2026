Masterclass: Generative AI. By Ben Urbain & Tim De Paepe

Do you want to learn what Generative AI is, what its applications are, and how you can get started with it? Then this Generative AI masterclass is for you.

Are you a creative director, art director, graphic designer, content creator, marketer, copywriter, or photographer, and do you want to learn what Generative AI is, what its applications are, and how you can get started with it yourself? Then this Generative AI masterclass is for you.

In the first sessions, we dive into the fundamental knowledge of what AI actually is, and we explain how neural networks work in an understandable way. You’ll receive up-to-date examples of AI usage, both in societal and business contexts. We’ll also address the ethical, legal, and environmental impacts of AI, giving you a holistic perspective on this rapidly evolving technology.

Next, we focus on generative AI in practice. You’ll learn how AI is used to generate content and discover the steps that lead to creating custom visual and textual output.

In the final sessions, we take it a step further: we introduce several advanced techniques in image generation using models like Stable Diffusion. Think of the visual interface ComfyUI, setting up image generation workflows, and leveraging techniques such as ControlNet, Image-To-Image, and LoRA to produce unique content. Whether you want to refine an existing image or incorporate your own styles and concepts, after this masterclass you’ll have a range of tools at your disposal to use AI the way you want.
