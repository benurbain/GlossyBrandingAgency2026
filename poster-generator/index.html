Glossy Poster Generator

Space: randomize · ← →: variations · P: proof sheet · G: grid

Randomize rolls everything again, the recipe included. The arrows step through variations with the settings below.

Text

Drag any text on the poster to move it, drag a corner to scale it. Arrow keys nudge, plus and minus scale, double-click puts it back.

System

On Random the roll decides. Pick a value to lock it for the variations. Wildness sets how often the roll interferes: stickers, tape, ghost shapes, flipped palettes, light weights and crooked printing.

Image

No image loaded. Drop a photo on the poster, or work without one: the generator then draws a shape of its own.

Your image stays in your browser: nothing is uploaded. Cut out loads a model on first use through @imgly/background-removal (AGPL).

Finish

Type

Schibsted Grotesk, variable from 400 to 900.

Export

SVG and PDF are vector, with the font embedded. PNG is 3000 px wide.
